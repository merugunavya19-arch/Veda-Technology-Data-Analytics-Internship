## 📊 Descriptive Statistics Analysis – Superstore Dataset
## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on performing **Descriptive Statistics** on the **Sample Superstore dataset** using **Microsoft Excel**.

The objective is to understand the distribution, central tendency, and variability of important numerical variables such as **Sales, Quantity, Discount, and Profit**.

## 🎯 Objectives
Calculate the **Mean** of numerical columns.
Calculate the **Median** and **Mode**.
Measure variability using **Standard Deviation**.
Find the **Minimum** and **Maximum values**.
Calculate the **25th, 50th, and 75th Percentiles**.
Understand the middle 50% of the data.
Compare Mean and Median to understand the distribution of the data.
Interpret the statistical results for business analysis.
## 📂 Dataset

The project uses the **Sample Superstore dataset**.

## Numerical Columns Analyzed
**Column**	     **Description**
Sales	        Sales amount for each order
Quantity	    Number of products ordered
Discount	    Discount applied to the order
Profit	      Profit or loss from the order
## 📊 Statistical Measures

The following measures were calculated:

**Mean** – Average value of the data.
**Median** – Middle value when the data is ordered.
**Mode** – Most frequently occurring value.
**Standard Deviation** – Measures the spread or variability of the data.
**Minimum** – Smallest value.
**Maximum** – Largest value.
**25th Percentile (Q1)** – 25% of observations are below this value.
**50th Percentile (Q2)** – Same as the median.
**75th Percentile (Q3)** – 75% of observations are below this value.

## 📈 Mean vs Median

Mean and Median were compared to understand the distribution of the numerical variables.

**Mean > Median** → may indicate right-skewed distribution.
**Mean < Median** → may indicate left-skewed distribution.
**Mean ≈ Median** → suggests a more balanced distribution.
## 📏 Standard Deviation

Standard deviation was used along with the mean to understand how widely the values are spread around the average.

A higher standard deviation indicates greater variability, while a lower standard deviation indicates that values are more concentrated around the mean.
