## 📊 First Chart Story – Turning Numbers into a Narrative
## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a **Data Visualization and Storytelling analysis using the Sample Superstore dataset** in Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to transform business data into simple charts and communicate meaningful insights through a clear visual story.

The analysis follows:

## Raw Data → PivotTables → Charts → Insights → Story

## 🎯 Objectives
Analyze sales performance across categories.
Understand monthly sales trends.
Compare sales performance across regions.
Analyze profit across product categories.
Create simple and meaningful charts.
Add descriptive chart titles and takeaways.
Present the analysis as a connected business story.

## 📊 Charts Created
## 1️⃣ Sales by Category

**Question**: Which category generates the highest sales?

A column chart is used to compare sales across:

Furniture
Office Supplies
Technology

## Takeaway:

Technology records the highest sales among the three product categories.

## 2️⃣ Monthly Sales Trend

**Question**: How do sales change over time?

A line chart is used to show monthly sales from **January to December**.

## Takeaway:

Monthly sales vary over time, showing changes in sales performance across different periods.

## 3️⃣ Sales by Region

**Question**: Which regions contribute to sales?

A bar chart is used to compare sales contribution across different regions.

## Takeaway:

Sales contribution differs across regions, helping identify regional performance patterns.

## 4️⃣ Profit by Category

**Question**: How does profit vary across categories?

A column chart is used to compare profit across product categories.

## Takeaway:

Profitability varies across categories, showing that sales volume and profit performance are not necessarily the same.

## 📖 Overall Story

The analysis shows how Superstore sales and profit performance vary across categories, regions, and months. Category analysis highlights differences in sales contribution, while the monthly sales trend shows changes in performance over time. Regional analysis shows differences in sales contribution across regions. Finally, profit by category provides additional insight into overall business performance.
